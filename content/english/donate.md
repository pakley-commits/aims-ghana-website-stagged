Gifts to AIMS Ghana support the students, teaching, research and outreach that bring Africa's brightest young minds into the mathematical sciences.

Online giving is not yet available on this website. To make a gift, or to talk about supporting a scholarship, a programme or an event, email [info@aims.edu.gh](mailto:info@aims.edu.gh) and our team will reply with the options.
