Ohemaa Akosua Ateah is a dedicated Senior Finance Officer at AIMS Ghana, where she efficiently manages financial operations, including financial reporting and supporting the audit process. She ensures compliance with financial regulations and supports the organization’s financial planning and analysis. Ohemaa began her career with internships at Societe Generale Bank and gained valuable experience during her national service in the Treasury Department of the Ministry of Environment, Science, Technology, and Information. She holds a BSc in Banking and Finance from Central University and an MSc in Accounting from the University of Ghana Business School and is currently preparing for her ICA Level 2 exams. Outside of work, she enjoys sightseeing, swimming, reading, and hiking.
