Seyram Kelvin Anku-Etu is a graduate of the Regional Maritime University – Ghana.

He is CISCO certified and his experience spins around management of Server Infrastructures and Data-Center operations across platforms (Unix, Linux and Windows), Network and System Administration, Cyber Security and Vulnerability Assessment.

Prior to joining AIMS Ghana, he worked with RPS Engineering Services as a Field Engineer and the National Lottery Authority as the Systems Administrator.

As the IT Manager, he is committed to delivering his optimum best to make AIMS-Ghana an excellent environment for both students and staff.
