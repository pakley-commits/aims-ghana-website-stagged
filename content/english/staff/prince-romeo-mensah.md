Romeo Mensah is a mathematician working in the field of mathematical fluid dynamics. His research combines techniques in classical and stochastic analysis to study partial differential equations arising from the dynamics of simple and complex fluids and their interactions with elastic structures. He holds a B.A. in Mathematics with Economics from the University of Ghana and master’s degrees from AIMS Ghana and Universität Bonn. Afterwards, he obtained a PhD at Heriot-Watt in Scotland, which was followed by several postdoctoral positions at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (G.S.S.I.) in Italy, Imperial College London in England, and Technische Universität Clausthal and Universität Duisburg-Essen in Germany.
