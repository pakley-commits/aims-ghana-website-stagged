John Appiah is a results-oriented communications specialist with a proven track record of developing and driving impactful communication strategies, executing successful communication campaigns and helping organizations build strong, sustainable and reputable brands. He brings on board experience and expertise in crafting compelling narratives, building strong stakeholder relationships and leveraging on data-driven insights to achieve measurable results.
John has over 15 years of experience having worked in senior communication roles in organizations including Invest in Africa, British High Commission, Intu-IT Ghana, Multimedia Group Limited, Media General Group Limited and Koforidua Clinic. John Appiah holds
Masters in Arts degree in Communication Studies and Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science & Sociology all from the University of Ghana, Legon. He is a 2016 Fellow with the Young African Leaders Initiative (YALI), Regional Leadership Center (RLC) West Africa.
