Prince is a seasoned brand and design specialist with over 13 years of experience in creating impactful print and web design solutions. His portfolio includes collaborations with Central University, the Chartered Global Investment Analyst Institute (CGIA), the Africa China Centre for Policy and Advisory, iSpace Foundation, Bethel Revival Choir, and Days for Girls Ghana. A tech-savvy designer with a strong interest in Al-driven design, Prince combines creativity with advanced technology to deliver engaging, brand-aligned solutions.

He holds a bachelor’s degree from the University of Cape Coast and a certificate from the National Film and Television Institute (NAFTI). Known for his innovative approach, Prince is dedicated to elevating brand presence through strategic and effective design.
